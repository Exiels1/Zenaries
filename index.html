How we work

Small on purpose.

There's no account manager, no handoff between departments, and no junior dev quietly learning on your budget. You talk to the person building your product, from the first message to the last deploy.

That's the trade-off of a studio this size — fewer projects running at once, and more attention on each one. We're new enough to still be careful about what we say yes to.
